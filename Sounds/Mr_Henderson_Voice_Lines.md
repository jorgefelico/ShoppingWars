# 🎙️ Mr. Henderson — Voice Acting Recording Script
### Character: Mr. Henderson (General Manager, Store #404)
### Game: *Shopping Wars*

---

## 📋 Voice Actor & Production Notes

### Character Profile
- **Age / Archetype:** 40s–50s overworked, underpaid retail General Manager. Corporate-brainwashed, passive-aggressive, and teetering on the verge of a nervous breakdown.
- **Vibe:** *Cave Johnson* (*Portal 2*) meets a tired, deadpan suburban department store manager. He treats brutal physical violence as an OSHA liability and an inventory clearance opportunity.
- **Delivery / Tone:**
  - **Deadpan / Monotone:** For routine store rules and casualty cleanups.
  - **Forced Corporate Cheer:** When promoting sales or announcing discounts.
  - **Manic / Panicked:** When store infrastructure fails (blackouts, sprinkler bursts, Groomba rampage).
  - **Smug / Greed:** When shoppers spend high amounts of money or get into bidding wars.
- **Audio Processing Tip:** Lines will be played over the supermarket ceiling speakers with a two-tone chime (`Sounds/store_chime.wav`), light bandpass radio filtering (300Hz–3.5kHz), and slight store reverb.
- **Recommended File Format:** `WAV` (44.1kHz or 48kHz, 16-bit or 24-bit, Mono, trimmed heads and tails).

---
